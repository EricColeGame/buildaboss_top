## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I retrieve my items if the boss does not spawn?
No. Once materials are deposited into the summon altar, they are consumed by the server script. If the server restarts before the meter reaches 100%, progression may reset depending on local database configurations.

### What is the most cost-effective item for build a boss how to spawn a boss?
Outbreak-specific trophies like Rat Tails generally offer the best point-to-value ratio. While high-tier trophies like Goblin Teeth provide slightly more points, their market value in raw emeralds makes them less economical to burn.

### Why did I not receive loot after the boss was defeated?
Most event arenas require you to deal a minimum amount of damage to qualify for the global drop table. If you stood too far away or arrived right as the boss died, the game will not register your participation.

### How often can players summon the boss in the festival zone?
While the altar accepts items continuously, internal cooldowns typically lock the meter for 15 to 30 minutes following a boss defeat. This buffer allows arena cleanup and ensures server performance remains stable.
