### Aesthetic Customization vs. Hitbox Alterations

Altering an enemy’s model does more than change visual appeal. Scaled body parts affect hit detection zones:

*   **Enlarged Head Models:** Increases the precision zone for critical strikes by roughly 30% to 45%, letting you maximize headshot bonuses.
*   **Armored Plating:** Shields specific zones, requiring you to aim for exposed limbs or maneuver around the target.
*   **Bulky Silhouettes:** Easier to hit with bouncing or scattering projectiles, though they often carry increased natural stagger resistance.

### Countering Defensive Shields

Android managers, mechanical bosses, and high-tier villains often deploy energy barriers when taking heavy burst damage. When a blue or gold barrier flares around your target, standard kinetic rounds ricochet without dealing damage. 

To overcome this mechanic, swap immediately to energy-disrupting armaments or timed area-of-effect explosives. Striking during the wind-up animation before the shield locks in can stagger the boss, canceling the barrier entirely.
