## What Is the Boss Challenge Mode in BTD6?

Introduced in Update 40.0 and expanded across subsequent patches, Boss Challenge is a dedicated game mode housed within the Events tab. It gives players full control to pick any Boss Bloon—such as Bloonarius, Lych, Vortex, Dreadbloon, Phayze, Blastapopoulos, or Diamondback—and pit it against any map in the game. You can challenge standard beginner courses like Monkey Meadow or test your limits on expert gauntlets like Bloody Puddles and Blons.

The primary role of this mode is practice, iteration, and personal high-score hunting. Unlike standard weekly events that impose strict tower restrictions and map limits, the default challenge sandbox keeps your tower and hero roster fully open. 

```
[Events Menu] ➔ [Boss Challenge Icon (Lower Left)] ➔ [Select Boss & Map] ➔ [Toggle Difficulty / Battle Mode]
```

## Configuring Your Build a Boss Custom Boss Challenge

Setting up an effective run requires understanding how the interface tracks progress and how competitive modifiers alter the playfield. You can run your game as a relaxed training exercise or turn it into a ranked personal trial by enabling Battle Mode.

### Battle Mode and Scoring Metrics

Checking the Battle Mode box on the setup menu completely strips away crutches like Double Cash Mode, Fast Track, and consumable Powers. It forces authentic tournament rules and tracks three distinct performance metrics. Whenever you launch a **build a boss custom boss challenge** under Battle Mode rules, the game evaluates your final tier completion first, meaning reaching Tier 5 at a slower speed always outranks a fast Tier 2 run.

| Scoring Metric | Measurement Basis | Ideal Practice Objective |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fastest Time** | Real-time seconds to eliminate tiers | Optimizing burst-DPS setups and Paragon abilities. |
| **Least Cash** | Total money spent on defenses | Trimming defensive bloat and maximizing farming ROI. |
| **Least Tiers** | Total upgrade tiers purchased across all monkeys | Finding hyper-efficient, consolidated tower upgrades. |

## The Economic Engine: Step-by-Step Boss Farming Blueprint

You cannot defeat upper-tier bosses on standard round income alone. Elite bosses require millions of dollars in defensive firepower by Round 120. A disciplined economic setup is mandatory to fuel your **build a boss custom boss challenge**.

```
R1–R39: 0-0-0 Farms ➔ 2-0-0 Farms ➔ Marketplaces / Banks
R40–R59: Build Tier 1 Defense ➔ Transition to 4-2-0 Banana Research Facilities
R60–R79: Overclock Central Farm ➔ Expand BRF Network ➔ Tier 2 Boss Cleanup
R80–R99: Deploy Banana Central (5-2-0) ➔ 10x Ultraboost Stacks ➔ Paragon 1 Ready
R100–R120: Mass Paragon Upgrades (Degrees 30–60+) ➔ VTSG Preparation
```

### Stage 1: Rounds 1 to 39 (Early Greed)

1. Place an efficient, cheap defense (such as a Free Dart Monkey, a Sub, or a Ninja Monkey) to clear early bloon waves without spending extra cash.
2. Place your first Banana Farm as soon as possible, upgrading it to 2-0-0 before starting your second farm.
3. Depending on your micro preference, transition into 3-2-0 Banana Plantations or 0-2-3 Marketplaces for easy resale value.
4. Prepare your Tier 1 defense around Round 38. A single 2-0-5 Elite Defender or an Overdrive/Tack Zone cluster placed near damage amplifiers (like a 4-2-0 Embrittlement or 3-0-0 Super Brittle) will dismantle the initial boss without demanding high investment.

### Stage 2: Rounds 40 to 79 (BRF Scaling)

1. Immediately sell redundant early defense towers the moment the Tier 1 boss pops. Reinvest every dollar into Banana Farms.
2. Build toward a 5-2-0 Banana Central while filling available water areas with Merchantmen if playing on aquatic maps.
3. Start deploying 4-2-0 Banana Research Facilities (BRFs).
4. Place an Engineer Monkey to build a 0-4-0 Overclock, keeping the Overclock buff active on your highest-earning farm around the clock.
5. For the Tier 2 boss at Round 60, deploy mid-cost powerhouses such as a 1-5-0 MAD (Moab Assured Destroyer) or a 5-0-2 Archmage paired with armor debuffs.

### Stage 3: Rounds 80 to 120 (Paragons and Late Game)

Community reports and top leaderboard clears confirm that surviving Elite Tier 4 and Tier 5 encounters requires heavy Paragon firepower. Normal difficulty can often be cleared with a single high-tier Paragon, but Elite bosses command astronomical health pools.

## Actionable Tips to Dominate Your Boss Challenges

1. **Exploit the Resale Value:** Never let boss-killer towers sit idle between rounds 40 and 60. Sell high-tier defense units immediately after the boss dies and reinvest those funds into income generation. 
2. **Track the Penalty Flashes:** In Update 56.0 and beyond, the game provides a visual queue: the penalty timer starts flashing 5 seconds before counting against your time score. Use this indicator to time your burst abilities.
3. **Account for Projectile Erasure:** Boss spawn entrances destroy incoming projectiles without triggering secondary fragment bursts. Do not place towers like the Mega Massive Munitions Factory directly at the spawn point; back them off slightly to keep your damage output consistent.
4. **Leverage Co-op Rules for Solo Play:** If practicing with friends, remember that Paragon placement restrictions are applied on a per-player basis rather than globally, making multi-lane defenses far more forgiving.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I earn Trophies or Monkey Money rewards in Boss Challenge mode?
No, the mode does not award event trophies, badges, or monkey money for completion. It is designed entirely as an unrestricted training environment to practice routes, benchmark farming setups, and set personal leaderboard records.

### Why is the Boss Bloon I want to practice locked or unavailable?
If a Boss Bloon is currently active in the weekly live event, that specific boss cannot be selected in the practice menu. Instead, the interface routes you directly to the live event. All non-featured bosses remain available to practice.

### How do I win a build a boss custom boss challenge match?
You win immediately upon destroying the Tier 5 boss, which spawns on Round 120. You do not need to finish the remaining rounds through Round 140 to claim victory.

### What causes an instant defeat during a boss tier?
You will fail the challenge under three conditions: your life total drops to zero, the boss completes the track and exits, or you take longer than 20 consecutive rounds to defeat an active boss tier.
